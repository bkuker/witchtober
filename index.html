The Harwell Decatron

  1. 1951

    The Harwell Decatron is completed for the British Atomic Energy Research Establishment.

  2. 1957

    The machine is at the end of its useful life, so it's given to the Wolverhampton and Staffordshire Technical College and renamed the Wolverhampton Instrument for Teaching Computing from Harwell.

  3. 1973

    It keeps teaching computing at Wolverhampton University until 1973.

  4. Today

    Still switched on. Still computing. 🕯️

The Wolverhampton Instrument for Teaching Computing from Harwell is currently the oldest operational digital computer in the world.
